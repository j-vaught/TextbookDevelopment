\caption{Comparison of convex and non-convex functions. \textbf{Left:} A convex function $f(x) = x^2$ (Garnet curve). The chord connecting any two points on the curve (Atlantic dashed line) lies above the curve, satisfying the convexity definition. There is a unique global minimum (Garnet dot). \textbf{Right:} A non-convex function $f(x) = x^4 - 2x^2 + 0.5$ (double-well potential). The chord can lie below portions of the curve. Multiple local minima (Horseshoe dots) exist, separated by a local maximum (Rose dot). Gradient descent paths (Congaree arrows) from different initial points converge to different local minima, illustrating why non-convex optimization is challenging.}
\label{fig:convex_nonconvex}
