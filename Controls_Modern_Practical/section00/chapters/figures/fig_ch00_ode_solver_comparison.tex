\caption{Comparison of numerical ODE solvers for the first-order linear equation $\dot{y} = -0.5y + 1$ with initial condition $y(0) = 0$. The exact solution $y(t) = 2(1 - \ee^{-0.5t})$ approaches the steady state $y_\infty = 2$. \textbf{Euler's method} (Garnet) exhibits first-order accuracy with visible deviation from the exact solution. \textbf{Heun's method} (Atlantic blue), also called the improved Euler or trapezoidal method, achieves second-order accuracy. \textbf{RK4} (Horseshoe green) provides fourth-order accuracy and is nearly indistinguishable from the exact solution even with a relatively large step size $h = 0.5$. The inset shows the absolute error versus time for each method.}
\label{fig:ode_solver_comparison}
