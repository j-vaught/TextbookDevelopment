\caption{The $z$-plane for discrete-time system analysis. \textbf{Top left:} The unit circle divides stable (Horseshoe shading, $|z|<1$) from unstable (Rose shading, $|z|>1$) regions. Atlantic crosses mark stable poles, Rose crosses mark unstable poles, and Honeycomb crosses mark marginally stable poles on the unit circle. \textbf{Bottom left:} Lines of constant damping ratio $\zeta$ in the $z$-plane are logarithmic spirals. Higher damping corresponds to trajectories closer to the positive real axis. \textbf{Bottom right:} Circles of constant magnitude $|z|$ correspond to constant settling time---smaller $|z|$ gives faster decay.}
\label{fig:z_plane}
\end{figure}
