\caption{Visualization of a constrained optimization problem. The feasible region (shaded in Atlantic blue) is the intersection of half-planes defined by the linear inequality constraints. The dashed Rose curves are level sets of the linear objective function $f(\vecx) = x_1 + 2x_2$, with lower values toward the origin. The optimal point $\vecx^* = (2.5, 0)$ (Garnet) occurs where the lowest level curve touches the feasible region boundary. At this vertex, the gradient $\nabla f$ points outward from the feasible region, satisfying the KKT conditions. The constraint $2x_1 + x_2 \leq 5$ is active at the optimum.}
