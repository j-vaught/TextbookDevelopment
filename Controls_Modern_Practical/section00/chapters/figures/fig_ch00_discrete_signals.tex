\caption{Common discrete-time sequences. (a) Unit impulse $\delta[n]$: a single nonzero value at $n=0$. (b) Unit step $u[n]$: constant value of 1 for $n \geq 0$. (c) Unit ramp $r[n] = nu[n]$: linearly increasing for $n \geq 0$. (d) Decaying exponentials $a^n$ with $|a| < 1$: smaller $|a|$ gives faster decay. (e) Discrete cosine: periodic with period $2\pi/\omega_0$ samples. (f) Damped sinusoid: exponential envelope times sinusoid. (g) Alternating sequence: corresponds to pole at negative real axis ($z < 0$). (h) Growing exponential with $|a| > 1$: represents unstable system response.}
\label{fig:discrete_signals}
