The complex conjugate poles (Garnet $\times$) lie on a circle of radius $\omega_n$ (Atlantic dashed). The damping ratio $\zeta$ equals the cosine of the angle $\theta$ from the negative real axis (Congaree dashed lines). The real part $\sigma = -\zeta\omega_n$ determines the decay rate, while the imaginary part $\omega_d = \omega_n\sqrt{1-\zeta^2}$ gives the damped oscillation frequency.
